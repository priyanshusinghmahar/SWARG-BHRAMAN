# DevBhoomi Experiences | Uttarakhand Travel, Heritage & Artisan Booking Platform

A modern, culturally authentic travel booking platform dedicated to **Devbhoomi Uttarakhand** (Garhwal & Kumaon), designed to bridge conscious travelers directly with hereditary mountain craftsmen, organic village homestays, and iconic regional experiences with **0% middleman fees**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features Built

### 1. 🎨 Direct Connection with Local Artisans & Craftsmen
- **Verified Master Artisans**:
  - **Meenakshi Joshi**: Traditional Aipan Folk Artist (Almora)
  - **Harish Tamta**: Hand-hammered Copperware Guild (Tamta Mohalla, Almora)
  - **Sunita Devi Bhotia**: Tribal Bhotia Sheep-Wool Handlooms & Carpets (Munsiyari)
  - **Trilok Singh Negi**: Traditional Garhwali Wood Carving / Likai (Tehri)
  - **Geeta Devi**: Eco-friendly Ringal Bamboo Basketry (Chamoli)
  - **Binsar Mahila Collective**: Wild Himalayan Jakhiya Spices & Buransh Flower Preserves
- **Direct Contact & Workshops**:
  - One-click **Call Studio** and **WhatsApp Direct Chat**
  - Book hands-on masterclasses (e.g. 3-hr Sacred Aipan Painting session)
  - Direct purchase of certified GI-tagged handcrafted creations

### 2. 🗺️ Interactive Devbhoomi Map & Local Directory
- Interactive OpenStreetMap/Leaflet integration centered on Uttarakhand
- Custom pins for **Famous Destinations**, **Artisans & Craft Studios**, **Food & Sweets**, and **Live Events**
- Popup previews with photos, phone contact, and Google Maps direct navigation
- Filter bar and interactive sidebar list with instant camera zoom (`flyTo`)

### 3. 🏔️ Famous Locations & Destinations
- Curated guides for **Rishikesh, Nainital, Auli, Chopta & Tungnath, Kedarnath Dham, Badrinath & Mana, Almora & Kasar Devi, Mussoorie & Landour, Jim Corbett, Valley of Flowers, Munsiyari, Kausani**
- Altitudes, best travel seasons, key highlights, and tour pricing

### 4. 🪔 Live Spiritual Ceremonies & Upcoming Fairs
- **Daily Live Ganga Aarti (Triveni Ghat, Rishikesh)**: Simulated live broadcast stream with audio visualizer and VIP seating reservation
- **Upcoming Cultural Fairs**: International Yoga Festival, Phool Dei Spring Festival, Nanda Devi Mahotsav, Auli Winter Ski Carnival, Harela Green Festival

### 5. 🍲 Iconic Food & Sweets Shops
- **Kheem Singh Mohan Singh** (World-famous Almora Bal Mithai & Singodi since 1865)
- **Chotiwala Restaurant** (Authentic Garhwali Thali in Rishikesh since 1958)
- **Prakash & Co.** (Sister's Bazaar, Landour since 1928)
- **Sakley's Mountain Bakery** (Nainital since 1944)
- **Himadri State Handloom Emporium** (Dehradun)
- **Pahadi Rasoi** (Bhatt ki Churkani & Mandua Roti)

### 6. 📸 Himalayan Photo Gallery
- Categorized photo showcase: High Peaks, Spiritual, Local Arts & Culture, Lakes & Valleys, Wildlife
- Interactive Lightbox with photographer credits and likes counter

### 7. ⭐ Traveler Reviews & Community Hub
- Verified traveler reviews with star breakdown and upvote helpful features
- Interactive **"Write a Review"** modal that dynamically updates the live platform

### 8. 🎟️ Comprehensive Booking & Checkout Flow
- Select dates, guests (adults & children), stay type (Pahadi Stone Homestay, Alpine Camp, Pine Chalet)
- Add-on cultural experiences: Aipan class, Pahadi cooking session, Bal Mithai souvenir box
- Instant booking ticket pass with generated Booking ID, QR code, and print/download pass options
- Confetti celebration upon confirmation

### 9. 🏛️ Three Dedicated Portals
1. **Traveler Portal (`My Trips`)**: Manage active bookings, view digital travel passes with QR codes, saved wishlist, and eco-credits.
2. **Vendor Portal (`Artisan Hub`)**: Switch between master artisans (Meenakshi, Harish, Sunita), toggle live studio open/closed status, manage product catalog, add new workshops, view direct traveler orders and sales analytics.
3. **Admin Portal (`Trust Console`)**: Platform overview (₹3.42 Cr direct economy, 4,821 bookings), pending artisan KYC verification queue with 1-click approvals, destination management, and AI planner telemetry.

## 🚀 Running the Project

```bash
# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start development server
npm run dev

# Build for production
npm run build
```

The application runs on `http://localhost:3000/`.
